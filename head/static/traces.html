Traces

Insights shows traces in the control panel but has no traces API, so Porthole cannot fetch them. It shows the trace ids produced by the tentacle request chains and its own spans, and links to the Traces tab where you can search for an id. How an application's traces reach Insights is not documented yet; this page says what was exported and where.
