Alerts

An alert rule watches one metric over a window and notifies channels when the threshold is crossed. Porthole reads the rules we created for the fleet, their instances (active and resolved) and the channels, including the webhook channel that points back at this site. Deliveries to that webhook appear below with their headers, so the undocumented signature scheme can be seen. Run the 'Alert round trip' voyage to watch the whole loop with timings.
